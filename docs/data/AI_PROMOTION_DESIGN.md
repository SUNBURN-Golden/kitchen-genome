## Where publication stops today

`kg_ai.review.publish()` appends a badge, quote, source, names and review identifiers to a restaurant or kitchen’s `ai_reviewed` array, or to `ai_reviewed_unlinked`. It folds `restaurant_text or org_text` into `restaurant` and drops the extracted role and tenure. Its duplicate check operates within one holder and can suppress a second publisher carrying the same wording. `Site.match()` can also attach a quote by chef alone. The holder is therefore a display location, insufficient evidence of employment. An empty queue returns from `review()` before reading the seed.

`kg_collect.site_publish.Corpus.rows()` accepts source-asserted rule extractions. `apply_ready()` publishes approved bundles and calls the G3 projection. G3 reads independently persisted career assertions. The review annotations have no path into either input. In `crawler/apply.py`, the creation counters come from reconciling restaurant `publication` markers against `crawler/applied.json`.

For November 21 KST, `data/ai_review_log.jsonl` records **88 approvals and 12 rejections**. Of the approvals, 38 appended an attached quote, 21 appended an unlinked quote, and 29 matched the duplicate check in `publish()`. That accounts for the increase from 20 to 41 unlinked. The daily pipeline log confirms an empty applied list and zero added, upgraded, ready, new verified and new checking.

## Promotion and identity

1. **One entry point for fresh approvals and backfill.** Run an offline promotion pass on every invocation of `kg_ai.review.review()`, including an empty queue or a paused model. Read attached and unlinked entries from a snapshot before moving anything. Adapt legacy `chef`, `restaurant`, `src`, `quote` and `date` to the same input as a newly approved extraction or standalone `candidate_fact`. Enrich by exact review/extraction ID when the original record survives. Treat `date` as the article publication date. Rejections, rollback tombstones and unresolved model findings never enter this pass. Preserve the reviewer’s original decision, verbatim quote, URL, timestamp and badge.
2. **Bind one stated relation.** Accept `employment_claim` and `career` as candidates for current or former work according to the wording, `opening` when the chef actually opened that restaurant, and `mentorship_claim` for training at an identified venue. Record a named mentor only when the source binds trainer and trainee. Use G3 span, language and field validation. Add narrowly tested Korean forms for explicit leadership, titled apposition and “출신”, including when the venue precedes the chef. The existing G3 verb matcher alone misses these constructions: the ko `current` cue matches `이끌고 있`/`총괄한다` but not the adnominal `이끄는` or `맡고 있는`, and `출신` is a `former` cue yet `_binds` requires a locative/object particle immediately after the venue, which “전 밍글스 출신 정한세” lacks. Require the named person or an attested equivalent spelling and the named venue in the quoted claim. Neither an article-wide name hit nor an annotation holder supplies a missing endpoint. Take role, dates and geography only when bound to the right stint and venue. A saved, previously approved exact quote can support checking when older fetch metadata is unavailable. Verification requires the independently checkable originals.
3. **Check the employer and the work separately.** A corporate promotion, hotel employer, school, contest, guest service, gala, pop-up or plan cannot establish a restaurant stint. A restaurant explicitly named inside a hotel can. An article about an award can explicitly identify a chef’s regular restaurant. A roster, a booking list such as “venue (name)”, or entrants meeting their mentors supplies insufficient evidence on its own. Apply the test to the named outlet and clause. In the 소재 quote the previous pop-up ended and the chef subsequently opened a named dining restaurant. A blanket keyword ban would lose that opening — the ko `reject` cue matches `팝업` anywhere in the clause, so `validate_item` discards the whole sentence even though the opening verb `오픈했` binds.
4. **Resolve before creating.** Use the venue part of `Site.match`, Gazetteer aliases, `target_entity`, legacy site IDs, and G3 `lineage_identity.resolve_branch/resolve_chef` with active redirects. Accept a kitchen and restaurant sharing a name as one site identity only when they map to the same branch. Use sourced bilingual spellings, name order, spacing, hyphens and romanization to find candidates. Use venue, role, dates, branch location or explicit bilingual evidence to select an identity under the G3 rules. An existing active support can supply same-branch identity context on replay. Hold unresolved homonyms or conflicting locations. A similar spelling alone cannot justify a redirect or a new duplicate. For an unknown-location venue scoped by G3 to one publisher, a second publisher needs evidence identifying the same branch before it can corroborate.
5. **Create only after binding succeeds.** In one transaction reuse or create the canonical SQLite `chef`, `restaurant` and `branch`, with supported aliases and target mappings. Reuse G3 stable identity and site-ID allocation. Track exactly which objects and redirects promotion created. Roll back provisional inserts on a failed resolution. An unmatched restaurant gets a durable `restaurants[]` holder with its stable route ID. Store G1 `branch.country_code` and `branch.city` independently when stated, with their sources. Omit unknown public values. A Korean name, cuisine, publisher or chef’s hometown supplies no venue geography.

Role and period can remain individually checking. Keep the AI badge on its source after corroboration.

## Replay, counters and withdrawal

- **Write and recover.** Use the existing daily lock and atomic seed replacement. Commit an approved review’s replayable publication input and delivery state in `review_queue.payload_json` with its decision. Use the same ownership receipt for identity changes. This closes the current failure window between closing a review in SQLite and writing the seed at end of run. Replay incomplete delivery by review ID and de-duplicate the JSONL decision by action/ID. Seed, audit and DB cannot share a transaction. Mark delivery complete after the seed and audit are durable. Preserve separate supports when the old quote-display deduper would collapse them. Iterate to stable identity/holder assignments within one run. A second run with identical inputs emits no transitions, timestamps, audit lines or file changes.
- **Count public changes.** Compare the effective graph before and after publication, including legacy scalar chef/venue pairs and projected edges. Key a link by canonical chef, employer, relation and compatible stint, with a persistent link ID across corroboration or alias redirects. Distinct roles/dates that identify separate tenures keep separate IDs. Count each link once across direct cards, origin paths and both review invocations. `AI_REVIEW promoted` counts newly visible links in that invocation and `unlinked_remaining` is the final list length. On this fixture the first pass expects `promoted=7 unlinked_remaining=37`, then `promoted=0 unlinked_remaining=37`.
- **Deliver the daily summary.** Store pending creation/upgrade events with their stable link ID, KST publication day, gate, restaurant ID and sources in the same seed replacement. Reconcile these alongside rule events in `crawler/apply.py`, including when the rule queue is empty. Use event IDs for reconciliation: `reconcile_events` currently keys `(restaurant_id, day_kst)`, so two promoted chefs at one restaurant on one day would collapse to one event. Fold both daily review passes into the unpublished batch. `added` counts new visible links, `ready` includes those eligible additions, and `new_alumni_checking` / `new_alumni_verified` split additions by their gate at publication. A previously published checking link becoming verified increments only `upgraded`. If both proofs arrive before the first daily publication, report one verified addition. Include creations in `applied` using the existing `new_alumni` event contract. A recoverable batch ID spanning audit and summary replacement prevents either a lost summary or a duplicate on retry.
- **Compute totals from the same public graph.** Include canonical IDs for projected chefs in `named_chefs`, including English-only names. Extend `verified_total` and `checking_total` with distinct evidenced links, reconciling equivalent legacy rows first. Judge the relation’s gate independently of optional role/period gaps. Retain legacy coverage for rows with no projected equivalent. `alumni_by_kitchen` counts distinct destination restaurants for actual origins, including secondary origins. A direct stint without a destination contributes to chef/link totals. Reuse the same canonical IDs for the scoreboard’s first transitions.
- **Withdraw one contribution.** Extend `scripts/ai_review_rollback.py` for all three selectors, review ID, extraction ID and `--since`. Under the same lock, remove matching annotations and support IDs, tombstone withdrawn reviews, and recompute affected fields, gates, paths and routes from surviving supports. Removing a verifying source can restore checking. Removing the last support deletes a promotion-only link. Prune a generated chef, venue, alias, or location only when no surviving review, native claim, curated reference or later edit owns it. Rehome surviving quote-only annotations to unlinked if their generated page disappears. With `--db`, reconcile owned identities and reverse solely owned G3 redirects. Retain immutable history wherever foreign keys require it, excluding retired promotion-only identities from active lookups and projection roots. With seed/log alone the withdrawal recomputes the affected public `career_projection` rows from surviving supports plus the cached native baseline (no DB needed, since `materialize` requires a connection and `apply_ready` returns early without one); the next DB-backed pass consumes the tombstone before materializing. The tombstone source is the `rollback` action lines in `data/ai_review_log.jsonl` (and `payload_json.ai_review.rolled_back` with `--db`): seed-only rollback leaves the DB review `resolved`/approved, so the promotion backfill must exclude any review/extraction ID tombstoned in the log, not just DB rejections. Audit reversals retain the original event history.
